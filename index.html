Safety first

When an app is not enough

Do not rely on this tool alone if symptoms are new, sudden, or severe.

  • Seek urgent medical assessment: sudden hearing loss or rapidly worsening hearing.
  • Get emergency help now: new face weakness, speech/vision changes, or severe uncontrolled dizziness.
  • Reach emergency or crisis support: if you feel unsafe, hopeless, or may harm yourself.
  • Arrange ENT/audiology evaluation: persistent heartbeat-like tinnitus, one-sided tinnitus, or unequal hearing.
